# Code of Conduct

AstroTouch exists to make astronomy accessible, and that starts with how we treat each other.

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as our code of conduct. In short:

* Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background, identity or ability.
* Give and accept feedback constructively; focus on what is best for the people who will use the models.
* Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not acceptable, in any project space.
* Respect people's privacy: don't share names, photos or details of anyone who tried the models without their consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/enzoperesafonso). Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially, and
the maintainer may take any action they consider appropriate, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
